# Brag Plan: BIYY Tech

## Inspection rubric

1. **What is the app?** BIYY Tech replaces the spreadsheets an SME actually runs on with a real database and a dashboard shaped for its trade, plus a website built around that business and ranked where customers search.
2. **Strongest claim.** "There is exactly one version of the file because there is no file." Runner-up: "Retire the spreadsheet. Keep the business."
3. **Visual hook.** The spreadsheet itself: paper-white grid, hairline rules, four tabs named `final`, `final_v2`, `FINAL_use_this`, and a `#REF!` cell. It dies on camera.
4. **What to show from the real UI.** The Costwise MVP dashboard (`C:\Users\ianga\costwise`): the Verdict card (health ring, "RM … a month is recoverable", three pillar bars), the four-up KPI row, and the "Needs attention" alert rows with their real rule copy.
5. **Shortest satisfying video.** 22s. Six scenes: spreadsheet, swap, dashboard working, three trades, website, demo CTA.
6. **Tone.** Preset `polished`. Direction: a quiet operator film. The product is somebody's payroll, not a joke.
7. **Audio.** Steady clean bed (vol-12), sparse motion-matched accents, music fades under the final CTA.
8. **Share caption.** Draft below.
9. **User flow worth showing.** Spreadsheet in → one dashboard that answers "is anything bleeding money" → the alert list ranked by money at stake → the same product laid out for a different trade. Website integration is the second product beat, not a second flow.

## What is this app?

BIYY Tech builds the dashboard and the database an SME runs on, then the website that gets it found. The dashboard in this video is the real MVP: Costwise, the restaurant/e-commerce/property build.

## The angle

Kill the spreadsheet on screen in the first three seconds, then spend the rest of the video showing what replaced it: a working dashboard that names the leak, prices it, and tells you what to do today. Not a diagram of a database. The database doing its job. Then the second product, a site built around that business, and the one thing the viewer is asked to do: book a free demo.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

A spreadsheet, white and hairline-ruled, filling in cell by cell. Four tabs along the bottom: `stock_final`, `stock_final_v2`, `stock_FINAL_use_this`, `stock_FINAL_use_this(2)`. One cell resolves to `#REF!`. The line lands over it: **Retire the spreadsheet.** Then, as the grid drops away: **Keep the business.**

## Key moments (the middle)

- The spreadsheet rows fall out of frame and the Verdict card lands in their place: health ring at 68, "RM 12,480 a month is recoverable", three pillar bars filling.
- Four KPI tiles arrive left to right with real labels: Net revenue, Operating profit, Food cost against target, Labour cost against target.
- Three "Needs attention" rows arrive one by one, each carrying a real alert from the rule book, ranked by money at stake: chicken breast usage above recipe, labour cost above target, cheaper quotes available.
- The same dashboard re-labels itself for three trades: Restaurants, E-commerce, Property management, each with its own blurb.
- A website page with its search listing above it: first result, and the page beneath it answering the question. Line: SEO, GEO and AEO built into every page.

## Outro / punchline

Ink ground, BIYY mark, and the only ask: **Book a free demo.** Under it, small and wide-tracked: THIRTY MINUTES, NO SLIDES.

## User flow worth showing

Entry: the spreadsheet the business is on today. Key action: the dashboard states the verdict and ranks what is leaking, by money. Result: the owner has one screen instead of nine files, and the same product reshaped for whatever trade they run.

## Tone

- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a quiet operator film, shot in the brand's own six colours
- Interpretation: three to four holds per scene, no more. Fast entrances (0.3-0.5s) with long settled holds. No bounce, no zooms, no confetti. The numbers do the talking and every line gets long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)

Two palettes, deliberately. Everything that speaks as BIYY uses the brand tokens. The dashboard scenes use Costwise's own product tokens, because that is what the product actually looks like.

**BIYY frame (title cards, trade scene, website scene, outro)**
- Background: `#ffffff` paper, `#0b0b0c` ink for the outro and the veil
- Accent: `#1b4de4`, second series `#4c79f2`
- Muted: `#9ca3af` · Hairline: `#e5e7eb`
- Display font: General Sans (shipped locally from `public/fonts/`), weights 400/500/600
- Body font: General Sans
- Radius: 999px pills or 2px panels. Nothing between. No gradients, no side-stripes, no texture.

**Costwise dashboard scenes**
- Surface `#f6f7f9`, cards `#ffffff`, sidebar `#0f1319`
- Text `#1b212a` headings, `#4d5a6e` body, `#64748b` labels
- Brand green `#1fa270`, series: revenue `#1b212a`, profit `#12825b`, cost `#f97316`, labour `#2563eb`, variance `#b45309`
- Tabular numerals everywhere

## Share copy (draft)

Retire the spreadsheet. Keep the business. BIYY Tech puts a real database under your SME and builds the dashboard your trade actually needs, live in two weeks.

## Audio direction

- Role: steady clean bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean, the polished pick)
- Music treatment: in at 0, volume 0.32, fade under the final CTA from ~19.5s so the last line lands in near-quiet
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Lock at most two strong cues: the spreadsheet-to-dashboard swap and the outro mark. Snap the three alert rows to the beat grid at every other beat so each line holds its read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Let the Verdict card's presence and the outro mark's weight breathe on RMS. No waveform, no equalizer, no pulsing text.
- SFX posture: sparse. Five cues at most across 22 seconds.
- Audio-coupled moments: the `#REF!` cell, the swap, each KPI tile, each alert row, the outro mark.
- Restraint rule: nothing comedic, nothing bright. This is somebody's payroll. If a cue draws attention to itself, cut it.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The spreadsheet — 3.4s
Paper-white grid, hairline rules, a header row and eight data rows filling in. Four sheet tabs along the bottom: `stock_final`, `stock_final_v2`, `stock_FINAL_use_this`, `stock_FINAL_use_this(2)`. One cell in the totals column resolves to `#REF!` in ink (red is a dashboard state in this brand, never a marketing colour). The frame then veils to ink and both hook lines land over the dimmed grid: **Retire the spreadsheet.** at 1.64s, **Keep the business.** at 2.19s, held to the cut.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Rows fill top to bottom, roughly 0.12s apart. The `#REF!` cell resolves last.
Audio intent: quiet, procedural, slightly wrong by the end.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft light accent on the `#REF!` resolve.
Music: steady bed, already running.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scenes 2 and 3 — one continuous dashboard clip, 3.3s to 12.05s

### Scene 2 — The swap — 4.2s
The grid drops out of frame downward. The Costwise Verdict card lands in its place on the `#f6f7f9` surface: health ring at 68 labelled ATTENTION, headline **RM 12,480 a month is recoverable**, subline "3 open issues across cost, inventory, labour. Biggest single leak: Chicken breast usage 8.2% above recipe at RM 2,140 a month." Three pillar bars fill underneath: Cost control, Inventory, Labour. Both hook lines have already landed in scene 1, so this scene is product only. A small meta pill in the corner reads SAMPLE DATA and stays for every dashboard scene.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Ring sweeps to 68, headline settles, pillar bars fill left to right 0.15s apart.
Audio intent: the moment of relief. One dry landing, then quiet.
Audio-coupled idea: a single soft impact on the card landing, beat-locked.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The dashboard working — 4.4s
Four KPI tiles arrive left to right: Net revenue RM 412,860 (+4.1%), Operating profit RM 58,240 (+2.6%), Food cost 33.8% against a 31% target (OVER TARGET), Labour cost 28.1% against a 27% target (AT LIMIT). Then the "Needs attention" panel: three rows arrive one by one, each with a severity dot, a title and its money.
- Chicken breast usage 8.2% above recipe · RM 2,140 a month
- Labour cost 1.1 pts above target · RM 1,890 a month
- RM 3,400 of cheaper quotes available
Eyebrow above the panel: RANKED BY MONEY AT STAKE.
Sequential/interaction: yes. Tiles 0.18s apart; alert rows on every other beat so each one holds its read.
Audio intent: things clicking into place, not celebrating.
Audio-coupled idea: a light card accent per alert row, first and last only if three feels busy.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Laid out for your industry — 3.6s
The dashboard drains to ink and three trade cards arrive on it one by one, each a 2px white panel with a zero-padded index and its own blurb. (Built as its own scene rather than a veil over the live dashboard: layering the cards on top left the dashboard's own copy sitting under them, which reads as unreadable stacked text.)
- **Restaurants** — Food and labour cost, menu margin, waste and purchasing across your outlets.
- **E-commerce** — Contribution margin per order and per SKU once ad spend, shipping and returns are counted.
- **Property** — Rent collected against rent billed, arrears by age, occupancy and NOI.
Line above them: **Laid out for your industry.**
Sequential/interaction: yes. Three cards, 0.35s apart, all three hold together for the last 1.2s.
Audio intent: three deliberate placements.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft drop per card.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — And the site that gets you found — 4.0s
Paper ground. A search field types out `barber shop near KLCC`, then a single result listing resolves under it with a title line, a URL line and a two-line answer. Beneath, three wide-tracked labels tick on: SEO · GEO · AEO. Line: **Built into every page.**
Sequential/interaction: yes. Query types character by character, result resolves, then the three labels tick 0.25s apart.
Audio intent: light, precise, over in four seconds.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks under the typed query, thinned out; one tick per label.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Book a free demo — 3.4s
Ink ground `#0b0b0c`. The BIYY mark (white, `public/brand/biyy-mark-white-512.png`) settles at 64px, then **Book a free demo.** in display weight, then a wide-tracked meta line: THIRTY MINUTES, NO SLIDES · KUALA LUMPUR. Music fades out under it.
Sequential/interaction: mark, line, meta, 0.4s apart, final hold 1.3s.
Audio intent: the room goes quiet and one thing is left on screen.
Audio-coupled idea: one deep, restrained accent as the mark lands, beat-locked; let it ring as the music falls away.
Transition mood: hold to black-ink end.

**Music mood for this video:** steady, clean, corporate-adjacent, never bright.
**Audio summary:** A calm bed runs the whole video, sparse accents mark the swap and the three trade cards, and everything drains away under the final CTA so the last line is read in near-silence.
